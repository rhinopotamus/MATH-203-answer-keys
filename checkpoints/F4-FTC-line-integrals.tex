\section{Checkpoint F4: The FTC for line integrals}

Consider the vector field \(\vec{G}(x,y) = \langle 3y^2 - \cos(x), 6xy+e^y\rangle\).

\begin{enumerate}
    
    \item Is \(\vec{G}\) a gradient field? That is, can you find a potential function $g(x, y)$ such that \(\vec{G} = \nabla g\)?
    \item Compute \(\int_C \vec{G}\cdot d\vec{r}\) where $C$ is the top half of the unit circle oriented from $(-1, 0)$ to $(1, 0)$.
    
\end{enumerate}
