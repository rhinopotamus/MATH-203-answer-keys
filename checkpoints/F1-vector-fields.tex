\section{Checkpoint F1: Vector fields}

The moral of this checkpoint is that technology makes exploring vector fields very easy.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Use CalcPlot3D or something similar to generate a graph of the 2D vector field \(\vec{F}(x,y) = \langle 2x - 3y, 4x - 2y\rangle\). Take a screenshot and put it in your submission.
    \item Maybe this vector field is just a special case of a more general thing \[\vec{F}(x,y) = \langle 2x - ay, bx - 2y\rangle.\] Explore how this vector field looks for a couple of different values of the constants $a$ and $b$. Maybe pick some where $a = b$, where $a < b$, where $a > b$. Take screenshots and include them in your submission.
    \item What kinds of general things can you say about the behavior of this vector field?
\end{enumerate}
